About MOOMEEN PRODUCTS

A Siddha Healthcare Journey Blended with Modern Science
OUR JOURNEY

Rooted in Tradition, Guided by
Science

For over 50 years, traditional Siddha healthcare has been practiced and shared with people through experience and generations of knowledge. With the vision of carrying this heritage forward to the next generation through a scientific and contemporary approach, MOOMEEN PRODUCTS was founded.

Siddha medicine is not merely a system of healthcare; it is a reflection of Tamil heritage, a way of life rooted in harmony with nature, and a symbol of traditional medical wisdom passed down through generations.

Respecting this heritage and preserving its fundamental principles, our mission is to combine traditional Siddha knowledge with modern scientific understanding and contemporary quality standards to create Siddha-based products that are relevant to today's lifestyle.

OUR APPROACH

From Tradition to a Scientific Approach...

MOOMEEN PRODUCTS was born from a commitment to understand the Siddha medical knowledge passed down through generations through formal education and clinical experience, and to bring this knowledge to people with confidence and responsibility.

Our foundation is supported by the knowledge and experience of doctors with formal education in Siddha medicine, teaching experience, and years of clinical practice.

OUR FOUNDATION

Medical Knowledge & Clinical Experience

Dr. Mohammed Fharook, MD (Siddha), and his wife, Dr. G. Rathiga, MD (Siddha), founded MOOMEEN PRODUCTS based on their medical knowledge, professional experience, and deep belief in the values of traditional Siddha medicine.

Both doctors have formally completed their undergraduate and postgraduate studies in Siddha medicine. They also gained three years of academic and teaching experience as Assistant Lecturers at a Siddha medical college.

In addition, their medical journey includes more than 10 years of direct clinical experience, understanding patients' healthcare needs and providing medical care.

By bringing together their education, teaching experience, clinical knowledge, and traditional wisdom, MOOMEEN PRODUCTS strives to develop quality Siddha-based products and become a name that people can trust.
